Gemma AI Telemetry & Log Analysis Disclaimer

Please review and consent to the data and connectivity requirements before enabling Gemma AI analysis for your sandbox sessions.

  • Explicit Data Scope
    Gemma AI will read, parse, and analyze the contents of your submitted files, scripts, and runtime event logs to inspect behavior and generate safety insights.
  • Network & Internet Connectivity
    An active internet connection is required to communicate with the Gemma AI processing service. Ensure your connection is stable while uploading or analyzing files.
  • Data Handling
    Do not process files containing confidential enterprise data, unencrypted production secrets, or sensitive personally identifiable information (PII) unless you have authorization to do so.
  • User Consent Requirement
    Establishes an explicit opt-in mechanism before any local file contents are read or transmitted to Gemma AI.
  • AI Insights Disclaimer
    Gemma AI provides automated heuristic analysis to highlight potential runtime risks. Results should be treated as advisory and verified by the developer.
  • Liability & Model Limits
    Clarifies that AI safety classifications do not replace manual security audits or automated sandbox isolation.
